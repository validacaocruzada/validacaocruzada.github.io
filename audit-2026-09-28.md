# xval.ai landing page — audit synthesis (2026-09-28)

Three independent reviewers (potential client / COO persona, landing-page CRO specialist, B2B business developer) audited source + live page, then cross-examined each other. Full reports: `local://audit-potentialclient.md`, `local://audit-landingspecialist.md`, `local://audit-bizdev.md` (session artifacts).

## Verdict (unanimous)

Delivery mechanics are unusually clear (fixed price, 2–6 weeks, success criteria, ownership, optional maintenance). The page fails on **evidence and identity**: no person, no client, no number, no price band, no GDPR posture, no legal entity. The client persona's decision after 90 s: "scroll, not book." Every reviewer ranked proof + accountable human as the #1 conversion lever, and all three moved the AOS/mobile CTA defect to the top after cross-talk.

## Verified technical defect (orchestrator-confirmed in source)

`aos/dist/aos.css` sets `[data-aos^=fade]{opacity:0}` unconditionally; `app/(default)/layout.tsx` inits AOS with `disable: 'phone'` (user-agent check, not viewport). Consequences:

- **No JS / JS error / slow script load → H1, subhead, both hero CTAs, all service cards, process cards and FAQs are invisible.** Only header and section headings survive. Static export makes this worse: HTML ships with `data-aos` attributes and the CSS rule already applied.
- **Real phones (phone UA):** content is `opacity:0` until hydration, then AOS strips the attributes → visible. Flash of empty hero on every mobile load.
- **Tablets and narrow desktop windows (non-phone UA, ≤ ~844 px tall):** hero CTA sits at y≈735 with AOS offset 120 → not triggered until scroll. Specialist observed this at 390×844 in desktop Chromium; on real iPhone/Android it is the flash case above, not permanent invisibility.
